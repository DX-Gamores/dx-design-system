<h3>When</h3>
<p>A specific day near today, where weekday context helps (due dates, bookings, report ranges). Birthdays and other far-away known dates → let people type (Date Field without the calendar, or three Text Fields). Relative periods (Last 7 days) → Segmented Control or Select presets.</p>
<h3>Property notes</h3>
<p>State (Default | Focus | Error | Disabled | Hover | Read-only) · Filled (False | True) · Size (Medium 40 | Small 32) · Label, Value, Helper text · Show label, Show helper, Show leading icon · Leading icon (swap). Range: same field, Value &quot;Nov 16 – Nov 21, 2026&quot;, placeholder &quot;Start – end date&quot;.</p>
<h3>Format</h3>
<p>Always show the format in the helper (mm/dd/yyyy) and accept typing; parse loosely (11/18, Nov 18), display unambiguously (Nov 18, 2026).</p>
<h3>Accessibility</h3>
<p>Input stays typeable; the calendar button is a separate button with aria-label &quot;Choose date, selected Nov 18, 2026&quot; and opens a dialog. Errors explain the rule (&quot;Pick a date after today&quot;).</p>
<h3>Tokens</h3>
<p>Same as Select; calendar icon in icon/secondary.</p>
