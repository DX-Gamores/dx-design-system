Content Guidelines

Words are interface. Clear first, then concise, then human.

Open in Figma

At a glance

3Voice rules · clear, concise, human
AaSentence case everywhere
0Periods on buttons, labels and titles
7 daysMax age for “2 hours ago” style dates

Clear → concise → human

In that order. A capable colleague: direct, calm, never cute at the user’s expense.

Clear✓“Your card was declined.”✗“Oops! Something went sideways with billing.”
Concise✓“Invite members”✗“Click here to invite new members to your workspace”
Human✓“We couldn’t save your changes. Check your connection and try again.”✗“Error 500: Request failed.”

Same word, same thing — everywhere

UseNotMeaning
Sign in · Sign outLog in · Login · Log outFor accounts
DeleteRemove (for permanent loss)Delete = gone for good (Danger tone)
RemoveDelete (for group membership)Taken out of a group; it still exists
Member · workspace · projectUser · team space · boardOur nouns for people, org and work
