Three parts, one job each

1TitleWhat’s empty, in plain words.e.g. “No projects yet”
2DescriptionWhy, or what happens next. One sentence.e.g. “Create a project to start organizing your team’s work.”
3ActionOne way forward — or none when empty is good news.e.g. “Create project”

From starter to shipped screen

  • Shells: src/templates/shell.tsx — AcmeShell (app) and AuthShell (sign-in, sign-up, invites).
  • Scenarios: src/templates/empty.tsx — the nine scenarios as data plus EmptyScreen; copy the words, swap the nouns.
  • In components: use <EmptyState> for full areas; for small containers keep the chrome and write one sentence.
